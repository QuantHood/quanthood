FEATURE 01 / 06 // POST-QUANTUM KEY

PQ key🔑

Generate a post-quantum key in your browser and commit its hash on-chain. The key never leaves your device — the chain only ever sees a 32-byte commitment, timestamped before the Moment.

SEED · 32 BYTES · CSPRNG
■ THE FLOW (01)
seed = 32B · crypto.getRandomValues sk, pk = ML-DSA-44.KeyGen(seed) // FIPS 204 pubHash = SHA-256(pk)[0..32] commit = QuanthoodArk.commit(pubHash)
CSPRNG 32B→ML-DSA-44.KeyGen→(sk, pk)
pubHash 32B→Ark.commit on-chain
Real ML-DSA-44 (NIST FIPS 204) — keygen, signing and verification all run in your browser via an audited implementation.
■ MECHANISM (02)
1Your browser draws 64 bytes of secure randomness (Web Crypto CSPRNG)
2Optional mixed mode folds in high-resolution timer entropy
3The ML-DSA-44 public key hashes to a 32-byte commitment — sent to QuanthoodArk on Robinhood Chain
4Everything stays in your browser: no transaction signs your key, no server, no seed phrase. Only the hash goes on-chain.
■ SIZES (03)

ML-DSA-44 signature2,420 B
ECDSA public key33 B
ECDSA signature65 B

Post-quantum keys are bigger — and prove ownership without elliptic curves. That hash, committed today, is what proves the account is yours after the Quanthood Moment.
