## Versi utama (matching struktur Qairos: intro + bullets + alamat)

Quanthood is live on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663).

Seven contracts that get your accounts ready for the quantum era — and one that watches for it:

◆ Oracle — on-chain threat level (0–5), rises on verified armed breaks
◆ Canary — a 12-rung ECDLP bounty ladder. A broken rung is an early warning; the contract pays the solver directly
◆ Ark — commit a post-quantum key hash (ML-DSA-44, FIPS 204) today, prove ownership with it later
◆ Guard — contracts that lock sensitive functions when the threat level rises, recovery through your PQ key
◆ Vault — a self-rescuing vault: when the threat trips, your ETH sweeps itself to your recovery address
◆ Market — bet on Quanthood's own outcomes: threat levels and canary breaks, settled trustlessly from on-chain data
◆ Insurance — parametric cover against the first armed break. Rate follows the live threat level; payout is automatic if a quantum computer proves itself in your term
◆ App — run it all from the browser, including an ECDLP solver you can attack the training rungs with

One rung already broken. One bounty already paid. On-chain, verifiable.
